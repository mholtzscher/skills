# Prove it works

Check the real artifact against the requested behavior. Read the published metadata, exercise the changed interaction, inspect the stored value, and check actual process state. Fresh-looking output, compilation, and self-reports are weaker evidence than observing the requirement directly.

When a check fails, verify that the observation measures the intended thing before changing production behavior. Keep a rerunnable check when it materially improves reliability. Report unverified claims as such.
