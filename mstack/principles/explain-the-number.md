# Explain the number

Before trusting a measurement, establish the workload, environment, baseline, and limiting resource. Check units, warmup, repeated samples, errors, and whether the measurement covers the user's actual path.

Distinguish noise from improvement. A microbenchmark does not establish end-to-end impact, and an eval score does not explain which behavior improved. Report the method and relevant uncertainty alongside the number; do not invent percentages for conceptual simplification.
