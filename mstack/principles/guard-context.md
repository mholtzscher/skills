# Guard context

Watch context pressure from large outputs, long files, repeated reads, and fan-out planning. Keep the lead's context focused on intent, decisions, evidence, and integration rather than raw payloads.

Size phases and cap their scope. Limit the files handled in a phase, set appropriate turn budgets, and account for mechanism costs such as dispatch, repeated loading, result collection, and integration. These are budgets for active work, not promises of a scheduler or unattended execution.

Give bulky history, logs, or independent research to bounded explorers. Request concise conclusions with source pointers rather than transcript dumps.

Pointers must identify both the source and when it matters. Read more to resolve a question that could change the decision. A summary must preserve uncertainty and contradictory evidence, not merely the favored answer. Keep enough source identity to retrieve the deciding evidence without repeatedly loading everything.
