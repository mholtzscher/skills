# Question the premise

When integrating a new requirement, consider the design as though that requirement had existed from the start. Compare that coherent shape with a local patch; choose based on the agreed scope and migration cost.

When two attempts fail the same gate, stop repeating their shared assumption. Identify all actors and state involved, reproduce the failure, and ask which premise the evidence contradicts. Trace root causes rather than adding guards that conceal symptoms. A larger redesign requires a revised approved sketch or spec.
